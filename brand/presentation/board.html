What we heard

The brief

A Mac shortcut that hears a song with Shazam, lifts it from YouTube and drops it into your Music library, tagged like a purchase. The name is a cheeky heist joke, so the mark should grin, not threaten: quick, mischievous, a bit outlaw, and simple enough for a 16 px browser tab.

cheekyquickmischievousbold

Success: the joke lands in half a second, it owes nothing to the real GTA logo, it reads at 16 px, works in one colour, and matches the app's heat-on-ink window.
